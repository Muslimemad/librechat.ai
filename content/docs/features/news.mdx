## Overview

News Intelligence monitors thousands of financial news sources and surfaces only what's relevant to your portfolio — with AI-generated sentiment scores and impact assessments.

## How It Works

1. **Ingest** — Continuous monitoring of news wires, SEC filings, earnings transcripts, and financial blogs
2. **Filter** — AI filters to news relevant to your holdings, watchlist, and interests
3. **Score** — Each article receives a sentiment score (-1.0 to +1.0) and impact rating
4. **Alert** — High-impact news triggers immediate notifications

## News Sources

- Major financial wire services (Reuters, AP, Bloomberg summaries)
- SEC EDGAR filings (8-K, 10-K, 10-Q, proxy statements)
- Earnings call transcripts
- Central bank communications (Fed, ECB, BoJ)
- Economic data releases

## Custom Alerts

Set up alerts for specific events:

```yaml
features:
  news:
    enabled: true
    alert_thresholds:
      sentiment_drop: -0.5    # alert when sentiment drops significantly
      impact: high            # only high-impact news
    sources:
      - sec_filings
      - earnings_transcripts
      - wire_services
    watchlist:
      - AAPL
      - MSFT
      - BTC
    topics:
      - interest_rates
      - inflation
      - AI_technology
```

## Integration with Portfolio

News Intelligence integrates with Portfolio Intelligence to show:

- How news events have historically affected your holdings
- Estimated price impact based on similar historical events
- Correlation between news sentiment and subsequent price moves

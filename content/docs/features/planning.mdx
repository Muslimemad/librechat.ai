## Overview

Financial Planning helps you build a structured roadmap to your wealth goals. Define your objectives, timeline, and risk tolerance — the AI models your path and adjusts as your situation changes.

## Goal Setting

Set multiple financial goals simultaneously:

- **Retirement** — target age, desired income, Social Security assumptions
- **Education** — college funding for children or grandchildren
- **Real Estate** — down payment savings timeline
- **Major Purchase** — vehicle, boat, business equipment
- **Custom** — any financial milestone you define

## Overview

Market Research gives you institutional-grade intelligence on any market, sector, or company — available through natural language chat.

## What You Can Research

### Company Analysis

Ask the AI to research any publicly traded company:

- Financial metrics (P/E, EV/EBITDA, revenue growth, margins)
- Competitive positioning and market share
- Management team and recent earnings commentary
- Analyst consensus and price targets

### Sector Intelligence

Get a macro view of any sector:

- Relative performance vs. market
- Key drivers and headwinds
- Top and bottom performing names

### Macro Research

- Interest rate environment analysis
- Economic indicator trends (CPI, jobs, PMI)
- Currency and commodity outlook

## AI-Powered Insights

The market research engine uses multi-step reasoning:

1. Retrieves real-time and historical data
2. Cross-references multiple data sources
3. Synthesizes findings into clear, actionable insights
4. Surfaces risks and opportunities with citations

## Configuration

```yaml
features:
  market_research:
    enabled: true
    data_providers:
      - source: financial_modeling_prep
        api_key: ${FMP_API_KEY}
    web_search: true
    cache_ttl: 300  # seconds
```

## Example Prompts

- "Compare NVDA vs AMD on revenue growth and margins for the last 4 quarters"
- "What are the biggest risks facing the regional banking sector right now?"
- "Summarize Tesla's Q3 2025 earnings call key takeaways"
- "Which S&P 500 sectors have the highest short interest?"

<Callout type="tip">
Enable [Web Search](/docs/features/web_search) to give market research access to live news and filings.
</Callout>

## Overview

The Investment Screener lets you find investment opportunities using plain English. Instead of learning complex filter syntax, just describe what you're looking for and the AI translates it into a precise query.

## Natural Language Screening

Simply describe the assets you want to find:

> "Show me profitable small-cap tech companies with revenue growth above 20% and a P/E below 30"

> "Find ETFs with expense ratios under 0.1% that track emerging markets"

> "Screen for dividend stocks with yields above 4%, payout ratios below 60%, and 10+ years of consecutive dividend growth"

## Supported Asset Classes

- **Equities** — US stocks, international stocks, ADRs
- **ETFs** — equity, bond, commodity, sector, thematic
- **REITs** — residential, commercial, industrial, healthcare
- **Crypto** — major tokens and DeFi protocols (where data available)

## Available Screening Criteria

### Fundamentals
- Revenue, earnings, margins, growth rates
- Valuation multiples (P/E, P/B, P/S, EV/EBITDA)
- Balance sheet metrics (debt-to-equity, current ratio)
- Return metrics (ROE, ROA, ROIC)

### Technical
- Price momentum (1M, 3M, 6M, 12M returns)
- Moving average relationships (50d, 200d)
- Relative strength vs. sector and market

### Dividend
- Dividend yield and payout ratio
- Dividend growth rate and streak
- Coverage ratio

### ESG
- ESG scores by provider
- Carbon intensity
- Board diversity metrics

## Saving Screeners

Save and schedule your screeners to run automatically:

```yaml
screeners:
  - name: "Quality Growth"
    schedule: "0 9 * * 1-5"  # 9am weekdays
    criteria: "profitable companies with revenue growth > 15% and debt/equity < 1"
    notify: true
```

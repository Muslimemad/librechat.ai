## Overview

Risk Analysis provides a comprehensive view of portfolio risk across multiple dimensions — from individual position risk to macroeconomic exposure.

## Risk Metrics

### Value at Risk (VaR)

The AI calculates portfolio VaR at configurable confidence intervals:

- **95% VaR**: Potential loss on 1 in 20 trading days
- **99% VaR**: Potential loss on 1 in 100 trading days

### Maximum Drawdown

- Historical maximum drawdown over selectable time periods
- Drawdown duration and recovery analysis
- Comparison to benchmark drawdowns

### Beta & Correlation

- Portfolio beta to broad market, sector, and factor indices
- Pairwise correlation matrix across holdings
- Hidden correlation clusters during market stress

## Drawdown Simulation

Run scenario-based stress tests on your portfolio:

```yaml
scenarios:
  - name: "2008 Financial Crisis"
    start_date: "2007-10-09"
    end_date: "2009-03-09"
  - name: "2020 COVID Crash"
    start_date: "2020-02-19"
    end_date: "2020-03-23"
  - name: "Custom"
    equity_shock: -30%
    bond_shock: +5%
    commodity_shock: -20%
```

## Hedging Strategies

Based on your risk analysis, the AI can suggest hedging approaches:

- **Options strategies** — protective puts, collars, covered calls
- **Inverse ETFs** — sector and broad market hedges
- **Asset allocation shifts** — moving to cash, gold, or treasuries
- **Geographic diversification** — reducing home country bias

## Configuration

```yaml
features:
  risk_analysis:
    enabled: true
    var_confidence: [0.95, 0.99]
    lookback_period: 252  # trading days
    benchmark: SPY
    stress_tests:
      enabled: true
      scenarios:
        - "2008"
        - "2020"
        - "custom"
```

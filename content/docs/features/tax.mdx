## Overview

Tax Optimization helps you keep more of what you earn. The AI continuously monitors your portfolio for tax-saving opportunities and alerts you before year-end deadlines.

## Tax-Loss Harvesting

The AI identifies positions with unrealized losses and suggests tax-loss harvesting trades:

1. Scans your taxable accounts for loss positions
2. Identifies suitable replacement securities (avoiding wash sales)
3. Calculates net tax benefit after transaction costs
4. Generates trade instructions for your broker

### Example

> You hold 100 shares of XYZ at a $4,200 loss. The AI suggests selling XYZ and buying a similar ETF to maintain market exposure while realizing the tax loss — saving an estimated $1,050 in federal taxes.

## Wash Sale Avoidance

The 30-day wash sale rule prevents you from buying back a "substantially identical" security within 30 days. The AI:

- Tracks all recent sales across accounts
- Flags purchases that would trigger a wash sale
- Suggests compliant replacement securities
- Monitors the 30-day waiting period

## Asset Location Optimization

Place the right assets in the right account types:

| Asset Type | Recommended Account |
|---|---|
| REITs, bonds, high-dividend stocks | Tax-deferred (IRA, 401k) |
| Growth stocks held long-term | Taxable brokerage |
| International stocks (foreign tax credit) | Taxable brokerage |
| High-yield bonds | Tax-deferred |

## Year-End Tax Planning

In Q4, the AI runs a comprehensive tax planning checklist:

- Realized gains/losses summary
- Remaining loss harvesting opportunities
- Roth conversion analysis
- Charitable giving optimization (bunching, DAFs)
- Required minimum distributions (RMDs)

## Configuration

```yaml
features:
  tax:
    enabled: true
    tax_year: 2026
    jurisdiction: US
    accounts:
      - type: taxable
        id: brokerage-001
      - type: traditional_ira
        id: ira-001
    harvesting:
      min_loss_threshold: 500   # minimum loss to trigger alert
      auto_scan: true
      scan_frequency: weekly
```
